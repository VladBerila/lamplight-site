Lamplight
A match-3 with a board you can turn

Match, turn the board, and relight the district.

Lamplight is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Match the pieces, turn the whole board a quarter to make them fall another way, and spend what you win restoring a small canal district, one lamp at a time, with the people who live there.
