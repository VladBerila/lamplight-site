Lamplight
A match-3 with a board you can turn

Stuck, lost something, or found a fault? Write to vladutz_b_05@yahoo.com?subject=Stillstone%20privacy. Telling me which device you play on and which place and level you were on helps a lot. I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Playing

How do I play? Swap two neighbouring pieces to line up three or more of a kind. The two keys beside the board turn it a quarter, and everything falls toward the new bottom; on most levels a turn costs one move. Each level shows what it asks for (pieces to collect, a lantern to bring out, ice to break) and how many moves you have.

What do the special pieces do? Four in a row makes a line lamp, which lights a whole row or column. A square of four makes a Moth, which flies to what the level needs most. Bigger matches make a bomb or a colour lamp. Swap two specials together for something bigger.

Boosters. The Hammer breaks one piece. The Swap exchanges two pieces. The Undo takes back your last move.

Is every level possible? Yes. Every level was solved by a computer before it shipped.

Lives

You have up to five lives, and one comes back every 20 minutes. You lose a life only by losing a level, or by leaving it after your first move (closing the game in the middle of a level counts as leaving it). Everything in the district stays open while you wait, and tonight's lamps never cost a life.

Purchases

I bought something and didn't get it. Close the game completely and open it again: the App Store sometimes confirms a purchase a little later, and the game picks it up at launch. If it is still missing, write to me with the date of the purchase.

I have a new phone. No Ads and the Starter Pack come back with Settings → Restore purchases. Your progress and coins are saved on the device only, so they do not follow you to a new one; coins and boosters already used cannot be restored.

Refunds. All payments go through Apple, so refunds do too: visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

You can choose to watch a short video for five more moves, a life, coins or a booster. From level 12 an ad may also appear after a win, at most once every three levels won and never after a lost level. No Ads and the Starter Pack remove the ads between levels; the videos you choose stay available.

Reminders

If you ask for them, the game can remind you when your lives are back or a gift is waiting, at most once a day. They are set on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. Switch them off in Settings, or in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Language and accessibility

The game follows your phone's language (English, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean and Portuguese); change it for this game alone in iOS Settings → Lamplight → Language. With VoiceOver on, the screens around the board are read aloud. Reduce Motion is respected.

Your data

The privacy policy says what the game sends and how to stop it. The short version: switch off Settings → Share gameplay statistics, and choose whether ads may be personalised when iOS asks.
